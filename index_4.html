Your claims

One row per plot of land you own. Enter the level and current monthly income exactly as the game shows it.
Where do these numbers come from?

Rock, Coal, Gold and Diamond claims earn a base monthly rent, and every level above 1 adds a flat +1% of that base (capped at level 100, which is 1.99× base). Enter the monthly income your claim is actually earning right now — the calculator works backward from that to figure out the per-level value, so it stays accurate even if your claim's base rent isn't exactly a community average. New claims are pre-filled with rough community-sourced starting rents you should overwrite once you see your own numbers.
